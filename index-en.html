DECOSHIP · Adhesive solutions

Personalise your boat… Make it speak.

Based in Canet-en-Roussillon, DECOSHIP Adhesive Solutions specialises in adhesive customisation for boats.

Our experience allows us to offer a wide range of services, from interior and exterior boat wrapping to simple adhesive lettering.

From design to installation…

We work in technical areas, boatyards and marinas throughout Occitanie, whether your boat is ashore or afloat.

DECOSHIP also supports businesses with their communication needs: logo creation, business cards and menus, vehicle graphics and advertising signs. We strive to meet every request.

We take particular care in selecting our materials and use premium-quality films exclusively.
